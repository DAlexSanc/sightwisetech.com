AI Vision

Inspection that learns your product

Our vision systems can be equipped with AI-assisted defect detection — trained on your parts, running at the inspection station, flagging what matters without slowing the line.

Onboarding
Ready in hours, not weeks
Bring a new product online with a small set of reference images. No labeling teams, no data-science project — just show the system what good looks like.
Consistency
The same standard every shift
Human inspectors tire and drift. An AI-assisted station applies the same criteria at 9am and 9pm, across operators, without fatigue.
Speed
Real-time at the station
Analysis happens locally, in the moment — a clear pass or fail with the defect highlighted, fast enough to keep pace with production.
Adaptability
Handles the unexpected
Beyond known defect types, the system can flag anomalies it hasn't seen before — the scratches, stains, and deviations that rule-based checks miss.
Privacy
Your data stays with you
Inspection runs on-site. Your product images and process data never have to leave the factory floor.
Documentation
A record of every part
Pass/fail results and defect maps can be captured for traceability — useful where audit trails and quality records are required.
How it works

From reference images to a working inspection station

01
Capture
We image your parts on the same optics you'll run in production, so the system learns under real conditions.
02
Train
A model is built around your product using our in-house toolchain — tuned to your defects and tolerances.
03
Deploy
The trained model runs locally at the inspection station, analyzing each part as it's presented.
04
Refine
As you run, the system can be retrained on new examples — accuracy improves with your process.
Is it right for you?

Where AI vision fits

High-volume, repetitive inspection
If operators are checking the same features on thousands of parts, automation pays off fast.
Cosmetic & surface defects
Scratches, stains, dents, and finish issues that are hard to specify with fixed rules.
Consistency requirements
When pass/fail decisions need to be identical regardless of who's on shift.
Frequent product changes
Fast onboarding means new products don't mean months of re-engineering.

AI vision is an option, not a requirement. Many of our systems ship as purely optical or digital inspection tools — and that's the right answer for plenty of applications.

Where it fits, we configure it around your process. Where it doesn't, we'll tell you. The specifics of our approach — models, hardware, and pipeline — we're happy to walk through directly with teams evaluating a deployment.
