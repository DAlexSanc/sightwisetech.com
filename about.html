Who we are

Built by engineers, for the factory floor

SightwiseTech was founded on the conviction that industrial inspection should be accessible, intelligent, and backed by people who have actually worked in manufacturing.

SightwiseTech grew out of nearly a decade of hands-on manufacturing experience — starting in field service and moving through automation, robotics, and systems integration.

That background made one thing clear: most manufacturers needed better visibility into their process, not more complexity. The tools on the market were too simple, too expensive, or too disconnected from real production.

We distribute and configure precision optical and digital inspection systems, and build our own software tools to complement them — designed to be used by engineers on the floor, not just specialists.

We serve the industries that define Mexican manufacturing: electronics, medical devices, aerospace, metal-mechanical, and automotive.
