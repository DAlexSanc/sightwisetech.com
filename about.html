Our story

From the Shop Floor
to the Startup

SightwiseTech grew out of nearly a decade of hands-on manufacturing experience — starting as a field service engineer and progressing through automation, robotics, and systems integration roles across the industry.

That background made one thing clear: most manufacturers needed better visibility into their process — not more complexity. The inspection tools on the market were either too simple, too expensive, or too disconnected from real production constraints.

We founded SightwiseTech in Guadalajara to change that. We distribute and configure precision optical and digital inspection systems, and we build our own AI-powered software tools to complement them — designed to be deployed and understood by engineers on the floor, not just specialists.

We serve the manufacturing industries that define the Jalisco and wider Mexican industrial economy: electronics, medical devices, aerospace, metal-mechanical, and automotive.
